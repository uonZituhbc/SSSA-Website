ABOUT THE ASSOCIATION

Together We Build a Stronger Science Community

The School of Science Students Association, commonly known as SSSA, brings together students from the School of Science at the University of Eldoret.

The association provides a platform through which students can participate in academic activities, leadership programmes, social initiatives, innovation projects and other activities that contribute to student development.

SSSA encourages students to work together, share ideas, support one another and actively participate in shaping their student experience.
