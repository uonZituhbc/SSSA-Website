ABOUT SSSA

A Community Built for Science Students

The School of Science Students Association (SSSA) is a student-led association serving students within the School of Science at the University of Eldoret.

The association provides a platform for students to interact, share knowledge, develop leadership skills, participate in academic activities and contribute positively to the university community.

Through collaboration and student participation, SSSA seeks to create an environment where every science student can learn, grow and make a meaningful contribution.

🔬

Our Purpose

To unite, empower and represent science students while promoting academic excellence, innovation, leadership and responsible student participation.
